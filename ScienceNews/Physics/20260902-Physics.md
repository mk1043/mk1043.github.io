Precise tests of this unitarity require independent determinations of its elements, such as | V us |, which governs the transition between strange and up quarks. Current measurements from kaon and tau decays show tensions that may hint at physics beyond the SM 1 , 2 , 3 . They were first introduced in [BATS07, BASTS10, Has07b] and found initial applications in constructing short quantum one-time pads [AS04], showing that the quantum entropy difference problem is QSZK-complete [BATS07], and giving examples of quantum states with decay of correla- tions and volume-law entanglement [Has07b]. While quantum expanders can be constructed if Ui is a random unitary [Has07a], there is also interest in efficient constructions where Ui is a circuit on n qubits of depth poly(n).
